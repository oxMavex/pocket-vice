# MODEL HEIST · the shared prompt (same text for every model)

Build a GTA-style open-city driving game that runs in the browser from ONE self-contained index.html.
Allowed: three.js r128 from cdnjs. Not allowed: any image, 3D model, font or audio files. Generate every texture, mesh and sound in code.

1. An original coastal city with its own name: street grid, sidewalks, working traffic lights, trees and palms, varied buildings with lit windows at night, a beach and waterfront.
2. A drivable car with real-feeling handling (acceleration, braking, drift, body roll), chase camera and hood camera, engine sound synthesized from RPM.
3. Day-to-night cycle: sunset, street lights, lit windows.
4. HUD: minimap with roads and mission markers, speedometer, money, mission text.
5. Two missions: a checkpoint sprint against a timer, and a taxi fare (pick up a passenger, drop off at a marker). Success and fail screens with a cash reward.
6. A garage where you can respray the car (color menu, money is deducted).
7. Ambient traffic that stops at red lights, and pedestrians on the sidewalks.
8. No weapons, no violence, no police.
9. Keyboard and mobile touch controls, 60 FPS target.
10. An autoplay demo mode (?demo) that drives through both missions and the garage for screen recording.
